> **Aura Bridal** is an editorial-grade luxury fashion boutique and bridal couture digital storefront. Crafted with **React**, **Lenis smooth scrolling**, **Framer Motion**, and **Swiper**, it delivers a cinematic, silky-smooth luxury browsing experience.

---

## ✨ Architectural Highlights

- 🌊 **Cinematic Lenis Smooth Scroll:** Hardware-accelerated inertial scrolling that mirrors high-end luxury fashion publications.
- 🎭 **Choreographed Micro-Animations:** Page transitions, staggered product cards, and hover parallax effects driven by Framer Motion.
- 📱 **Fluid Carousel Carousels:** Multi-touch gesture product sliders powered by Swiper.js.
- ⚡ **Lightweight Global State:** Real-time bag & wishlist state powered by Zustand.
